A vision became a brand.
Passion became a success story.

Every great story begins with an idea. Ours began with the courage to build something of our own, push boundaries and turn passion into something exceptional.

What began as a clothing shop and a vision grew under the leadership of founder Mads Kristensen into an international tattoo brand. From the first steps in Sønderborg, Denmark, to numerous studios across Europe, Tattoo Fashion now stands for creativity, quality and a clear philosophy: Tattoos are more than art on skin – they are an expression of personality, freedom and individuality.

FROM PASSION TO SUCCESS.

Tattoo Fashion was founded to take tattooing to the next level: not simply creating tattoos, but creating places where art, lifestyle and people come together.

Through passion, determination and unwavering belief in the vision, one studio grew into a brand that now brings people together across national borders.

The focus is not simply on growth, but above all on what makes Tattoo Fashion special: exceptional artists, custom designs, the highest quality standards and an atmosphere where everyone feels welcome.

MORE THAN A TATTOO.
A LIFESTYLE.

To us, every tattoo is unique. Every design tells a story, holds a memory or carries a personal meaning. That is why Tattoo Fashion is about more than the finished artwork – it is about the whole experience.

Our studios are places where ideas become reality, creativity knows no limits and imagination becomes one-of-a-kind artwork.

What drives us is our passion for what we do. What connects us is our love of art. And what defines us is the courage to keep moving forward.

Our story started small. Our vision has always been big.
