Website hosting

This website is delivered through GitHub Pages. Technical connection data, including your IP address, is transmitted to the hosting infrastructure when you access the site. According to GitHub documentation, IP addresses may be logged for security purposes. This processing serves the provision, stability and security of the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Actual storage periods and any transfers depend on the provider.

Google Fonts

The Manrope font is currently loaded from Google services at fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Google may receive your IP address when these resources are requested. The purpose is consistent presentation. The legal basis for loading fonts remotely must be assessed before final approval; hosting the fonts locally is a more privacy-preserving alternative.

Studio map and third-party libraries

The studio page loads the Leaflet library from unpkg.com and map tiles from OpenStreetMap-based map services. These providers receive technical connection data, including IP addresses. The map displays studio locations. The particular tile services, applicable legal basis and any required consent must be verified against the live website.

Contacting us

The contact form prepares an email draft in your device’s email application and does not itself send the message to our web server. If you send an email, we process the information you provide to respond to your request, based on Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for pre-contractual correspondence or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for other enquiries.

External links and social media

This site links to Instagram, studio sites and external shopping pages. A simple link does not itself send your data to the destination merely by being displayed. When you follow the link, the destination provider’s privacy terms apply.

Retention periods

We retain enquiries only as long as needed to handle them or as required by statutory retention obligations. Actual deletion schedules and hosting log retention must still be confirmed with the relevant providers.

International transfers

GitHub, Google and external mapping/CDN providers may process personal data outside the European Economic Area. Applicable safeguards, such as adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses, must be verified for the actual providers and contracts.

Your data protection rights

Subject to statutory requirements, you have rights of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21). Contact us using the email address above.

Right to lodge a complaint

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. For a company based in Schleswig-Holstein, the Independent Centre for Privacy Protection Schleswig-Holstein (ULD) may be relevant: datenschutzzentrum.de.

Privacy policy verification

This statement reflects the website functions identifiable in the source code. Hosting and email arrangements, actual deletion periods, and any additional services need to be checked against the real operation before final legal approval.
